Concept
<p>
A 2D sidescroller hack-n-slash metroidvania where the player will defeat enemies and bosses while exploring the world and finding more about how the world came to be what it is today. The player will have
multiple abilities such as elemental powers and equipment that will help them conquer obstacles and perform satisfying combos on enemies.

Lore
<p>
The world was at first chaotic and elemental energy bursts created life and harsh environments. Humankind was weak to these elemental disasters 
but brought to balance using an apparatus called the Aegis, the Aegis can seal excess bursts of energy from the planet, making areas habitable. 
Now, a great evil has plunged the planet into chaos, breaking the Aegis seals and gaining dominion over specific regions of the world.
</p>
Story

<p>
Girl inherits dead knight’s will to save the planet and bring balance to the elemental forces, but has conflict fulfilling that wish with her own goals.
She will traverse through many regions of the world while gaining elemental powers. But will she actually use them to save the world?
</p>
